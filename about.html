About the Founder – Amee Atit Shah

AA LUXE was founded by Amee Atit Shah, a passionate traveller with a deep love for discovering new places, experiencing different cultures, and creating unforgettable memories along the way.

"For Amee, travelling is more than just visiting a destination—it is about exploring, experiencing, connecting, and coming back with stories to tell."

Her curiosity to see the world and her passion for travel inspired her to turn her love for exploring into a journey of helping others experience travel in the best possible way.
